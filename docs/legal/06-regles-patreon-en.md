## 1. What the Kalendrath Patreon is

1.1. Kalendrath is an independent project, developed in the open by [À COMPLÉTER : prénom et nom] EI, trading as "Kalendrath" ("the Publisher"). The Patreon ([À COMPLÉTER : URL]) lets those who believe in the project **support its development**.

1.2. Support on Patreon is **support for the project**, not the purchase of the game, of access or of an in-game advantage. In particular:
- **Patreon does not give access to the beta** or to any test phase: alpha and beta access comes through tester waves and pre-registration, open to everyone;
- **Patreon gives no Kaelite and no power advantage** in the game;
- Patreon is not an investment, a loan or a pre-sale: it gives no right to any share of revenue or to a guaranteed release date. It gives no right to a refund of months of support whose benefits have been delivered. *[Revue: IM-13]*

1.3. Developing a game involves risks: delays, changes of direction, discontinuation. We regularly publish progress (a devlog every two weeks) and what the Patreon funds, so that you support us knowingly.

## 2. Who does what

2.1. **Patreon**, Patreon, Inc., manages your membership, payments, billing, the VAT applicable to your support, cancellations and refunds, under its own terms of use and refund policy. Payment disputes are handled by Patreon.

2.2. **The Publisher** provides the benefits described below and is responsible for delivering them. These rules apply in addition to Patreon's terms; in case of conflict, Patreon's terms prevail for everything concerning the platform and payment.

3.2. **Discord role and channel.** They are granted by linking your Patreon account to your Discord account through Patreon's official integration. They are removed at the end of the paid period.

3.3. **Credits.** Your name appears in the credits only with your consent, under the name or username you choose, and if it complies with the naming rules of the [terms of use](04-cgu-en.md) (Article 7.14). Once added, it stays in the credits even if you stop supporting, unless you ask for its removal.

3.4. **Votes.** Votes are advisory: the Publisher takes them into account and publishes the result, but keeps the final decision, in particular for lore consistency.

3.5. **In-game cosmetics.** They are delivered through a personal code to enter in the lobby, or by linking your Patreon account to your game account. They are tied to your game account, **non-tradable, non-resellable, without monetary value**, and give no gameplay advantage. They are kept after the beta reset and if you stop supporting. They are governed by the terms of use (Article 6.3).

3.6. **Timing.** Digital benefits are delivered within 7 days of the first successful payment; in-game benefits as soon as the corresponding feature exists in the game, which the tier page states clearly.

3.7. **No random draws.** In line with Patreon's rules and French law (a lottery reserved for paying participants is prohibited: Articles L322-1 and L322-2 of the French Internal Security Code), we run no lottery or paid draw among supporters. *[Revue: MI-9]* Any giveaway is open to everyone, supporters or not, under published rules.

3.8. **No vouchers or Kaelite.** In line with Patreon's rules, we give no vouchers or discount codes. By choice, we give no Kaelite either. *[Revue: MI-8]*

## 4. Changing and ending support

4.1. You may change or stop your support at any time on Patreon. Time-based benefits (role, channel, early access) end at the end of the paid period. Acquired benefits (credits, cosmetics) remain acquired.

4.2. If we must change or withdraw a benefit, we announce it at least 30 days in advance on Patreon, so you can change tier or stop supporting before the next period.

4.3. If the project stops, we announce it on Patreon, pause supporter billing through Patreon before the next cycle, and acquired benefits remain granted.

4.4. If a promised benefit has not been delivered, write to contact@kalendrath.com: we deliver it or, if that is impossible, offer an equivalent benefit; failing that, you may request a refund of the part of your support that corresponds to the benefit not delivered. Other payment refund requests are handled by Patreon. *[Revue: IM-13]*

## 5. Conduct

5.1. Supporter-only spaces follow the [terms of use](04-cgu-en.md) and the Discord rules. Supporting the project neither protects from nor aggravates any sanction.

5.2. A supporter banned from the game or the Discord may lose access to the spaces concerned; we then end their membership from Patreon so that billing stops, and refund the current period if a time-based benefit is withdrawn. We charge nothing ourselves. *[Revue: IM-13]*

5.3. Reselling a benefit, sharing early-access content before publication, or promising in-game benefits in exchange for Patreon support made on behalf of a third party is prohibited.

## 6. Personal data

Patreon is the controller for your Patreon account and payment data. We receive from Patreon your Patreon name or username, email address, tier and support status, in order to deliver your benefits. Details in the [privacy policy](02-politique-de-confidentialite-en.md), section 2.7.

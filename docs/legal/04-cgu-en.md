| N0 | Informal reminder | Message from the Team, no disciplinary record |
| N1 | Warning | Reasoned notice, kept on record for 90 calendar days |
| N2 | Temporary restriction | Removal of a feature (chat, transfers, API, purchases, rankings, participation in a war ranking or reward) for 24 hours to 14 days |
| N3 | Suspension | Account inaccessible for 3 to 30 days |
| N4 | Server ban | Character closed on the Server concerned, then deleted once the appeal and complaint periods have expired; other Servers remain accessible |
| N5 | Account ban | Permanent closure of the Account and all its Characters |

Serious breaches (payment fraud, account sale, targeted harassment, threats, security breaches, resale of cheating tools) may be sanctioned directly at the appropriate level. 10.3. The appeal may confirm, reduce, cancel or requalify the sanction. If cancelled, the record is cleared, removed benefits are restored and proportionate compensation is granted.

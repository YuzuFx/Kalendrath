## 1. The principle

A cookie, or more broadly a tracker, is information stored in or read from your browser (cookie file, local storage, fingerprint, pixel). Article 82 of French Law No. 78-17 of 6 January 1978 (French Data Protection Act), which transposes Article 5(3) of Directive 2002/58/EC ("ePrivacy"), requires your consent before any tracker is stored or read, **unless** its sole purpose is to enable electronic communication, or it is strictly necessary to provide a service you have expressly requested.

**Kalendrath only uses trackers that are exempt from consent.** No advertising trackers, no embedded social networks that follow you, no audience measurement tool that sets a cookie. That is why no consent banner is displayed. If we ever add a tracker that requires consent, we will ask for it through a banner allowing you to accept or refuse just as easily, and this policy will be updated beforehand.

## 2. Trackers used

### 2.1 Showcase site (`kalendrath.com`)

| Name | Type | Set by | Purpose | Duration | Consent |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `lang` | Local storage | Kalendrath | Remembering the language you chose (English or French) | Until you clear it | Not required: necessary for the requested service (language choice) |
| `__cf_bm` | Cookie | Cloudflare | Distinguishing humans from malicious bots, protecting the site | 30 minutes | Not required: service security |
| Anti-bot check cookies (Cloudflare Turnstile), on form pages only | Cookie or technical storage | Cloudflare | Checking that a form is submitted by a human, without advertising tracking | Duration of the check | Not required: security of the form you submit |

**Audience measurement.** The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which **sets and reads no cookie** and produces only aggregated statistics, for our sole use, with no cross-site tracking of individuals. It meets the CNIL's exemption conditions for audience measurement tools: purpose strictly limited to measuring traffic, anonymous statistical data, no combination with other processing, retention of no more than 25 months.

### 2.2 Lobby and game servers (`lobby.kalendrath.com`, `sN.kalendrath.com`)

| Name | Type | Set by | Purpose | Duration | Consent |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `kalendrath_session` | Cookie | Kalendrath | Keeping you signed in during your game session | 12 hours, renewed with each activity | Not required: authentication |
| `XSRF-TOKEN` | Cookie | Kalendrath | Protecting your actions against cross-site request forgery | Session duration | Not required: security |
| `remember_web_…` | Cookie | Kalendrath | Keeping you signed in between visits, **only if you tick "Stay signed in"** | 13 months at most | Not required: expressly requested service |
| Interface preferences (language, theme, filters, sorting, open panels) | Local storage | Kalendrath | Remembering your display settings | Until you clear it | Not required: interface customisation you requested |
| `__cf_bm` | Cookie | Cloudflare | Protection against malicious bots | 30 minutes | Not required: security |

### 2.3 Payment page

When you make a purchase, the payment page or window is provided by our payment provider, Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Limited) *[Màj 31/10]*. It sets trackers strictly necessary to complete the payment and prevent fraud, under its own responsibility; they are described in its policy: https://stripe.com/legal/cookies-policy [À VÉRIFIER : lien à jour].

### 2.4 Third-party platform content

We do not embed any YouTube video, X post or Discord widget that loads automatically. When a video or third-party content is offered, it is first shown as a still image: the platform receives nothing until you click to start it. By clicking, you accept that platform's trackers, governed by its own policy. Plain links to Discord, Reddit, X, YouTube or Patreon set no tracker on our pages.

## 3. What you can do

- Clear local storage and cookies in your browser settings. The trackers listed above are essential: blocking them may prevent you from signing in or playing.
- Leave "Stay signed in" unticked: only the session cookie will be used.
- Set your browser to refuse third-party cookies: this does not affect the game.

Help pages of the main browsers: Firefox (support.mozilla.org), Chrome (support.google.com/chrome), Safari (support.apple.com), Edge (support.microsoft.com).

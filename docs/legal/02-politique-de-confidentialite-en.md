This policy explains which personal data we process when you visit the Kalendrath website, leave your email address, play, buy Kaelite, support us on Patreon or join our Discord; why we do so; how long we keep it; and how to exercise your rights. It complies with Articles 12 to 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of 27 April 2016 (GDPR) and French Law No. 78-17 of 6 January 1978 on information technology, data files and civil liberties (French Data Protection Act).

**In short.** We collect the minimum. We do not sell your data and show no targeted advertising. The showcase site sets no analytics cookies. No severe sanction rests on a purely automated decision. Any question: privacy@kalendrath.com.

| | |

## 6. Security

We apply measures appropriate to the risk (Article 32 GDPR): encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, hashed API keys, administration access protected by two-factor authentication, limited moderator permissions, encrypted backups, regular security updates, logging of administrative access. In the event of a personal data breach likely to result in a risk to you, we notify the CNIL within 72 hours and, if the risk is high, we inform you directly (Articles 33 and 34 GDPR).

## 7. Your rights

You have the following rights:

- **access** to your data and a copy of it (Article 15 GDPR);
- **rectification** of inaccurate data (Article 16);
- **erasure** (Article 17), subject to data we must keep under a legal obligation (accounting, identification data) or for the establishment of legal claims;
- **restriction** of processing (Article 18);
- **portability** of the data you provided to us and that is processed on the basis of the contract or consent (Article 20);
- **objection** to processing based on our legitimate interest, on grounds relating to your particular situation (Article 21);
- **withdrawal of consent** at any time, without affecting prior processing (Article 7(3));
- **instructions** on what happens to your data after your death (Article 85 of the French Data Protection Act).

**How to exercise them.** By email to privacy@kalendrath.com, preferably from the address linked to your account, or from your account settings (data download, account deletion). We may ask for additional information to verify your identity, never more than necessary. We reply within one month, extendable by two months for a complex request, in which case we tell you within the first month (Article 12(3) GDPR). Exercising your rights is free of charge.

**Complaints.** If you believe your rights are not respected, you may lodge a complaint with the Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés (CNIL), 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France, www.cnil.fr, or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence in the European Union (Article 77 GDPR).

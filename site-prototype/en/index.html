Kalendrath

Three thousand years after the Sundering, the Nine Spheres are barely rising from the ashes. Raise your city, send your heroes against what stalks beyond the walls, and grow an empire.

Long-form strategy and empire management in your browser, designed first for PvE and cooperation.

What awaits you

A hero leads your empire.

An archetype, talents, gear forged by your own hand. A failure costs you time, never the hero.

A new world every chapter.

A new server opens with each chapter, on a fixed schedule.

PvP is never forced on you.

A peaceful player, or one without an alliance, is never crushed by someone stronger.

A city that grows

Your city starts humble and changes its face as its Town Hall rises: homes multiply, defenses go up, then magic and power take hold.

Town Hall tier shown

The obelisk, inherited from Kaelhelm, never moves. Everything else, you build.

A broken world

The world rests on the Straumar, lines of mana that run through reality like a living network.

Millennia ago, the scholars of Kaelhelm tried to align the Straumar of several Spheres. The city vanished, the continent shattered into Shards, and the Abyss was left in its place.

Today the hordes are stirring. You hold a human city, in the shadow of an obelisk no one yet understands.
